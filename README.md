## About

> ⭐ Star this repo if it helps you

I'm a CS senior graduating in December 2026, and I got tired of checking a bunch of different repos every day just to see what was new. So I built this. It pulls the major internship and new grad lists together, removes the duplicates, and posts new roles to my Discord as soon as they show up. It isn't only SWE either: front end, data/AI, quant, product, and hardware all get their own section.

The table updates automatically every 15 minutes and shows roles posted in the last 30 days, newest first.

**Want alerts in your own Discord server?** Fork this repo and follow [SETUP.md](SETUP.md). It takes about 5 minutes and it's free.
